# Bundled English dictionary

`en.aff`, `en.dic`, and `LICENSE` are unchanged copies of the bundled dictionary in [rubocop-spellcheck](https://github.com/dblock/rubocop-spellcheck). Their upstream source is [kotoshu/dictionaries at commit `744120eb38a485a64c9185496c8422f66c491c05`](https://github.com/kotoshu/dictionaries/tree/744120eb38a485a64c9185496c8422f66c491c05/en/spelling).

This is the SCOWL-derived American English Hunspell dictionary, version 2020.12.07 (size 60). Redistribution terms and notices are reproduced in full in [LICENSE](LICENSE); the app's MIT license does not replace them. No dictionaries are downloaded at runtime.
